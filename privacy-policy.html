Snooker Scorekeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Data Collection

Snooker Scorekeeper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All match data (scores, player names, game events) is stored locally on your device and never shared with us or any third party.

Local Storage

The app saves match history to your device's local file system using standard iOS data persistence (JSON files in the app's Documents directory). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it via iOS share sheet.

No Internet Access

Snooker Scorekeeper does not connect to the internet, send analytics, or display advertisements. It functions entirely offline.

Third-Party Services

This app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.

Your Data, Your Control

You can delete all stored match data at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Clear All Records" button).

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the GitHub repository: github.com/Pakerton-tech/ios-snooker
